About Me

Engineering, on and off the court

I am an engineering student from Germany completing my Bachelors in Mechanical Engineering & Robotics in Boston. I'm about to start my senior year.

Having interned at Siemens in Summer 2025 in the Industrial Metaverse Lab as an Automation Engineer, I was surrounded by Digital Twins, Virtual Reality, and various robotic hardware, immersed in the future of today's technology. This experience is what led me to find my calling in the automation industry.

To build on this experience, I joined Amazon as an Operations Engineer Intern for Summer 2026, where I'm working with large scale automation systems in fulfillment centers and also with a really cool robot — Vulcan.

Besides academics, I keep up my passion for tennis by practicing with the MIT Tennis team, and stay active through running — I just ran my PR (1:29h) in the half marathon in St Petersburg, Florida.

Stay a while and explore my projects where I turn ideas into real-world systems through CAD, simulation-based analysis, and hands-on assembly.
